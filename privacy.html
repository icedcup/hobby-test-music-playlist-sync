Privacy policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026.

Robin Music Playlist Sync is a personal, self-hosted tool that updates two YouTube Music playlists from ListenBrainz recommendations.

Data used

The app requests the Google YouTube account management permission. The installed program uses it to identify the owner's playlists, read playlist items, and add or remove items only in its managed Daily Jams and Weekly Jams playlists. It reads ListenBrainz recommendations and searches YouTube Music by song title and artist. It does not download audio.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials, playlist identifiers, track metadata, sync logs, and old playlist snapshots are stored on the owner's server in owner-only files. No credentials or account data are stored in the public repository. The tool sends requests to Google/YouTube and ListenBrainz for synchronization. It does not sell data, serve ads, or use analytics.

Retention and control

Local records remain until the owner removes them. The owner can stop the sync, revoke Google access in Google Account settings, and remove the local authorization and data files. Revoking access prevents future YouTube account access.

Questions can be raised through GitHub Issues.
